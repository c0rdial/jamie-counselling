## Overview

Serene and sophisticated with an organic, wellness-focused personality. The design feels spacious and calming with warm earth tones creating a sense of comfort and trust. Medium density layout with generous whitespace promotes mindfulness and clarity.

Asymmetrical grid system with generous margins and section spacing. Content is organized in clear visual hierarchies with ample breathing room. The layout emphasizes horizontal flow with split-screen hero sections and well-defined content blocks.

Uses a 2px base grid with scale: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7.5, 9, 10, 12, 12.5, 15, 20, 25, 35.

## Colors
- **Warm Cream** (#f8f6e8): Primary background color for main content areas
- **Rich Brown** (#312b24): Primary text color and navigation elements
- **Medium Gray** (#4f4f4f): Secondary text color for body content
- **Deep Forest** (#1a312a): Primary button background and accent elements
- **Sage Green** (#d1ea67): Accent color for highlights and interactive elements
- **Pure White** (#ffffff): Secondary background and card surfaces
- **Muted Teal** (#254a3f): Secondary button and subtle accent color
- **Pure Black** (#000000): High contrast text and strong emphasis

## Typography
- **Headline Font**: Lora
- **Body Font**: Inter
- **Label Font**: Manrope

The typography system creates elegant hierarchy through the pairing of Lora serif for headings with Inter sans-serif for body text. Lora headings use varied weights (400-500) with tight letter-spacing (-1.5px to -0.32px) for sophistication, while Inter body text maintains excellent readability with subtle negative letter-spacing (-0.16px). The scale relationships follow a harmonious progression from 16px body text up to 50px hero headings, with Manrope providing a modern touch for interactive elements.

## Elevation

Subtle depth created through soft shadows and layered rounded elements rather than sharp contrasts. The design uses gentle elevation with organic corner radii (12px-30px) and minimal shadow effects to create a floating, peaceful aesthetic.

## Components
- **Hero Section**: Large hero area with split layout featuring headline text on left and portrait image on right with rounded corners
- **Navigation Bar**: Clean horizontal navigation with logo on left and menu items on right, minimal styling with hover states
- **Primary Button**: Rounded button with dark forest green background, white text, and subtle shadow for primary actions
- **Image Card**: Rounded corner image containers with soft shadows and organic feel
- **Text Block**: Typography-focused content blocks with generous spacing and serif headings paired with sans-serif body text

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do use generous whitespace to maintain the calming, spacious feeling
- Don't use sharp corners or harsh shadows that break the organic aesthetic
- Do pair Lora serif headings with Inter body text for optimal hierarchy
- Don't exceed 3 font weights per family to maintain visual consistency
- Do use the warm cream background as the primary canvas color
- Don't introduce colors outside the earth-tone palette without careful consideration
- Do maintain consistent 12-30px border radius range for visual harmony
- Don't use pure black text except for high-emphasis content
